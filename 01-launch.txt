Intent
Open your phone on purpose.
Most of the time we pick up our phones out of habit, not because something needs us.
We check about twice as often as we think.
Limits people set for themselves while calm cut their social media time by about a sixth in a large experiment.
Intent keeps your tools one tap away and puts a short pause, and the minutes you choose, in front of the apps you want to use less. Calls, messages, payments, maps, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and hotspot keep working exactly as before.
